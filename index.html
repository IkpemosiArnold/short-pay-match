Short-Pay Match Sample data

Every credit points at a signed docket.

A made-up produce wholesaler's November payments, matched in plain code to the October statement, the signed dockets and what each café wrote.

Checking

A café that pays less than its statement has to say why. A deduction is credited only when the signed docket for that delivery notes the same item short, in the same unit. The credit is the quantity the docket notes, times the price on the invoice.

Anything else waits for you with the reason: damage the docket does not mention, more taken off than the docket notes, a payer name not on file, one payment for two statements, or an invoice paid twice. Nothing is guessed. Every credit waits for your approval.

For your accounts

Approved credit notes and the payments they settle, as a plain CSV. It is a generic layout, not any accounting system's own import format.
